Common questions

I bought pearls but they haven't arrived

Pearls are added as soon as Apple confirms the purchase. If the game was closed or lost connection, open it again and the purchase is delivered automatically. Purchases waiting for Ask to Buy approval arrive once they're approved.

Can I get my purchases on a new device?

Pearl packs and the Premium Season Pass are used up in your game, so there is no Restore button. Your progress, including what you bought, moves to a new iPhone or iPad when you restore that device from an iCloud or computer backup.

How do I ask for a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I catch the mystical giants?

Buy Glow Worms in Shop → Bait, choose them with the bait button under Cast, then cast. Each Glow Worm cast lures one of six giants, including the Megalodon and the Kraken.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. The game saves automatically on your device after every catch and purchase. No account is needed.

How long does the Premium Season Pass last?

Premium lasts for the current monthly season. A new season starts at the beginning of each month, and every reward you claimed stays yours.

There's no sound

Check the speaker button at the top right of the game, and make sure your device's silent switch is off. Game sounds follow the silent switch so they never interrupt your own music.

Requirements

iPhone or iPad with iOS 16 or later. The game works offline.

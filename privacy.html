Fisherman Go

Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

In short: Fisherman Go does not collect, store or share any personal information. Your game progress stays on your own device, and purchases are handled entirely by Apple.

Who we are

Fisherman Go is a fishing game for iPhone and iPad made by Velmont Digital ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no online features. It does not send any data to us or to anyone else, and it works fully offline.

Information stored on your device

  • Your progress (fish in your notebook, cash, pearls, upgrades, items, lodge and settings) is saved only on your device, inside the app's own storage.
  • Your device may include this data in its iCloud or computer backups, based on your own backup settings. We can't see those backups.
  • Deleting the app deletes this data from your device.

In-app purchases

Pearl packs and the Premium Season Pass are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment. We never receive your name, Apple Account, email address or payment details. The game receives a transaction number from Apple so it can deliver your item, and keeps that number only on your device to make sure each purchase is delivered once. Apple's privacy policy covers your purchase: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Photos

Photo mode lets you take a picture of your fishing scene. The picture is only saved or shared when you tap Save or share and pick where it goes. If you choose to save it to your photo library, iOS asks for permission to add the photo. The game never reads your photo library.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

The game shows no ads and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we do not collect information from children under 13.

Your choices

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can remove all game data at any time by deleting the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
